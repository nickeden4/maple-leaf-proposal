CRM recommendation

We can support HubSpot or Pipedrive. We will not make the pitch about either.

Deeper TRC expertise

HubSpot

Best fit if Maple Leaf wants marketing automation, richer lifecycle reporting, scalable segmentation, multi-team workflows and a platform that can grow into wider customer engagement.

Lean sales pipeline

Pipedrive

Best fit if the near-term requirement is simple pipeline visibility, enquiry ownership and sales activity tracking with a lighter operational footprint.

Our commitment: help Maple Leaf make the right CRM decision, configure it around the group operating model, and support adoption regardless of the platform selected.

Pipedrive figures are converted from published USD pricing using 1 USD = approx. £0.7493 on 9 June 2026. Final comparison will need confirmed user count, add-on limits, VAT and UK billing currency.

First 90 days

A practical route from audit to adoption.

Weeks 1-2

Discovery and system map

Interview key stakeholders, document systems, enquiry paths, data sources, current reporting and venue-level pain points.

Weeks 3-4

CRM decision and blueprint

Compare HubSpot and Pipedrive against Maple Leaf's required workflows, then produce the data model, pipeline structure and integration plan.

Weeks 5-8

Build, connect and test

Configure CRM foundations, forms, routing, automations, dashboards and priority integrations, then test with real enquiry scenarios.

Weeks 9-12

Enablement and optimisation

Train venue and group teams, refine reporting, tighten follow-up routines and identify the next revenue operations improvements.

Expected outcome

Maple Leaf gets a clearer route from customer intent to revenue action.

The end goal is not a prettier database. It is fewer missed enquiries, better cross-venue visibility, stronger campaign attribution, cleaner ownership, and a repeatable way to grow visitor revenue, events, lessons and membership without burying teams in admin.
